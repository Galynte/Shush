# Shush privacy policy

**Effective date: October 23, 2026**

Shush is a white-noise player. It runs on the phone and does not have an account.

## Information we collect

Shush does not collect personal information. It does not use analytics, advertising, or a server. The app does not request internet access.

## What stays on the phone

Shush saves a few settings in local storage so the next launch opens where you left off:

- the sound you selected (white, pink, or brown)
- the volume
- the sleep-timer choice, including a custom length

An in-progress playback session is stored only so the sound can continue if Android recreates the process. That session is not included in Android backup or device transfer. Uninstalling Shush removes all of it.

The sound itself is generated while it plays. Nothing is recorded.

## Permissions

- **Notifications** — the playback controls (play, pause, stop) and the time remaining. The notification channel is silent.
- **Foreground service** — keeps the sound playing with the screen off. Android shows this as the playback notification.
- **Wake lock** — keeps the processor from sleeping while sound is playing, and is released when playback stops or pauses.

You can deny notifications. Playback still starts; the shade controls may be hidden by the system.

## Children

Shush does not collect data from anyone, including children. It is a local sound player.
